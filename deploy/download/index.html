Installing

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details then Install anyway. It's a test build, so it isn't on the Play Store.
  4. To update, download again and install over the top. Your name and duck drawing are kept.

If the app says it needs an update, come back here for the newest build.

No Android phone? Play in the browser.
